# Power BI Task Guide

1. Import Customers.csv, Products.csv and Sales_Clean.csv.
2. In Power Query inspect Sales_Raw, handle missing Quantity/Discount, remove duplicates and verify data types.
3. Create the two one-to-many relationships in Data_Model.md.
4. Create the DAX measures in DAX_Measures.md.
5. Create: column chart (Sales by Category), line chart (Sales by Month), donut chart (Sales by Region), table/matrix (Product/Quantity/Sales), and KPI cards.
6. Build one dashboard with 3+ KPI cards, 4+ visuals and slicers for Region and Category.
7. Write 5 data-supported insights and 2 recommendations.
